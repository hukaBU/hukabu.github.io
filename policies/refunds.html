Refund and cancellation policy

Orders kept aside

You can cancel an order kept aside up to 12 hours before the pickup day, by email, at no cost. Orders are paid only when you pick them up, so there is nothing to refund.

Subscriptions

Tell us before the 25th of the month to stop or change a subscription: the change applies from the following month. A month already paid is not refunded, and bread not picked up by Saturday closing time is given to the local food bank.

A problem with a product

If something you bought is not right, bring it back or send us a photo within two days with your receipt. We replace it or refund it, as you prefer.

Questions about this policy: write to us from the contact page.
