Weekly bread subscriptions

A subscription keeps your bread aside every Saturday. You pay once a month in the shop, and it costs less than buying the same bread loaf by loaf.

PlanEvery SaturdayPrice per month
Weekly LoafOne sourdough loaf€24 (instead of €26)
Family BoxOne sourdough loaf, one rye bread and six butter croissants€80 (instead of €85.20)

How it works

  • Pick up your bread on Saturday between 7:00 and 19:00. What is not picked up by closing time is given to the local food bank and is not refunded.
  • A month counts four Saturdays. In a month with five Saturdays, the fifth one is free.
  • To stop or change your plan, tell us before the 25th: the change applies from the next month. The details are in our refund and cancellation policy.

To start a subscription, come to the shop or write to us from the contact page.
