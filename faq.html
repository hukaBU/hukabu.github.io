Frequently asked questions

Can I order online?

No. Send us an email at least 24 hours ahead with the products and the pickup day, and we keep them aside until closing time that day.

Do you deliver?

No, everything is picked up in the shop at 12 Example Street.

Which products contain allergens?

Every product contains gluten. The croissants also contain milk and egg, and the almond cake contains nuts, milk and egg. All products are made in the same kitchen, so traces of milk, egg and almonds are possible in all of them. Each product page lists its ingredients.

How do I pay?

In the shop, by card or cash, when you pick up. Subscriptions are paid at the start of each month.

Can I stop my subscription?

Yes, at any time before the 25th of the month, for the following month. See the refund and cancellation policy.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 7:00 to 19:00, and Sunday, 8:00 to 13:00. Closed on Monday.

Another question? Write to us from the contact page.
