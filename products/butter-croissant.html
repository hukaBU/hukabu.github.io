Butter croissant

€1.60 · 70 g · baked every opening day, first batch at 7:00, second at 10:00.

Made with butter only, never margarine. The dough is folded by hand three times and rests overnight, so the layers stay light and crisp. Best eaten the same morning; a few minutes in a hot oven bring a day-old croissant back.

Ingredients

Wheat flour, butter (28%), whole milk, sugar, yeast, salt, egg for the glaze.

Allergens

Contains gluten (wheat), milk and egg. Made in a kitchen that also uses almonds.

How to get it

Buy it in the shop or ask us by email to keep some aside a day ahead. Six croissants each week are part of the Family Box subscription. Back to the shop.
