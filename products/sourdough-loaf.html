Sourdough loaf

€6.50 · 800 g · baked every opening day, ready from 7:00.

Our everyday bread. The dough rises for 18 hours with our own rye starter, then bakes for 50 minutes in a stone oven. The crust is thick and dark; the crumb is open and slightly sour. It keeps for four days in a cotton bag, and freezes well sliced.

Ingredients

Wheat flour (88%), water, rye flour, sea salt. No yeast, no additives.

Allergens

Contains gluten (wheat, rye). Made in a kitchen that also uses milk, eggs and almonds.

How to get it

Buy it in the shop, ask us by email to keep one aside a day ahead, or take it every week with a subscription. Back to the shop.
