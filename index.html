Bread baked every morning in Demo Town

Hukabu Bakery is a small neighbourhood bakery. We bake sourdough loaves, rye bread, butter croissants and one cake of the day, every morning from 4 a.m., with flour milled less than 60 kilometres away.

Everything is sold in the shop or kept aside for you if you ask a day ahead. We do not deliver: you pick up your bread at 12 Example Street, Tuesday to Sunday.

What you can buy

  • Sourdough loaf, 800 g, €6.50, our everyday bread.
  • Butter croissant, €1.60, made with butter only, no margarine.
  • Rye bread, 600 g, €5.20, and a slice of almond cake, €3.80.

See the whole range with prices in the shop.

Weekly bread subscriptions

If you take the same bread every week, a subscription keeps it aside for you every Saturday and costs less than buying it loaf by loaf. You can stop it before the 25th of any month.

Opening hours

Tuesday to Saturday, 7:00 to 19:00. Sunday, 8:00 to 13:00. Closed on Monday.

Questions about allergens, orders or pickup are answered in the FAQ, and you can reach us through the contact page.
